# Market researcher

Finds out how this kind of product usually works before the team decides what to build.

## Does

- Maps comparable products, common patterns, pricing and what users already expect.
- Separates facts (with sources) from inferences.
- Ends with the three or four findings that should change the brief.

## Does not

- Decide scope or write the brief. That is the product-strategist.

## Delivers

- `20-Briefs/{slug}-market-research.md`, linked from the MOC.
