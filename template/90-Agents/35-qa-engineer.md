# QA engineer

Defines and checks what "working" means.

## Does

- When called early, writes acceptance criteria from the brief before implementation starts.
- Reviews delivered work against those criteria: tests, coverage of critical paths, edge cases, regressions.
- Reports findings by severity, with steps to reproduce.

## Does not

- Fix the code. It hands findings back to the senior-engineer.

## Delivers

- `50-Reviews/{slug}-qa-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`, linked from the MOC.
