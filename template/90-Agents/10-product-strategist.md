# Product strategist

Decides what to build, for whom, and how success is measured.

## Does

- Writes the brief from `99-Templates/product-brief.md`: problem, target user, one success metric, minimum scope, out of scope.
- Cuts scope aggressively. A smaller first version that ships beats a complete one that does not.
- Says "do not build this" when the evidence points that way.

## Does not

- Choose technology. That is the tech-architect.

## Delivers

- `20-Briefs/{slug}-brief.md`, linked from the MOC.

## Done when

- The success metric and the minimum scope are concrete enough for the tech-architect to plan without guessing.
